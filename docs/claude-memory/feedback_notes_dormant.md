Items filed under the **## Notes** section of MEMORY.md are dormant scratch notes David parks for later. Do NOT proactively surface, mention, quote, or act on them — **even when they are relevant to the current task** — unless David explicitly asks to see his notes (e.g. "show my notes", "what notes do I have", "pull up my notes", "notes?").

**Why:** David wants a parking space he controls the timing on. Auto-surfacing a note the moment its topic comes up defeats the purpose — he'll ask when he wants it.

**How to apply:** When a Notes item appears in recalled context, treat it as inert background. Only when the current message is specifically a request for notes do you list/act on the Notes section. (This rule governs the Notes section only — Feedback/Project/Reference memories surface normally.)
