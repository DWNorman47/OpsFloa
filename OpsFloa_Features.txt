OPSFLOA — FULL FEATURE LIST
For marketing reference
================================================================================


OVERVIEW
--------
OpsFloa is a cloud-based operations platform for construction contractors and
trades businesses. It started as time tracking and field documentation and now
runs the whole operation — workers clocking in on a job site, admins approving
hours and running payroll, estimating and bidding work, managing projects and
subcontractors, taking off quantities from plans, and billing clients — all from
a phone or desktop, online or offline.

The platform is built as a base of connected modules — Time Clock, Field,
Projects & Office, Scheduling/Dispatch, and Estimating & Billing — with
specialized, higher-value tool add-ons (Plan Room and the Takeoff layer) that
layer on top. Everything is accessible from the same login, on any device.


================================================================================
TIME TRACKING — WORKER SIDE
================================================================================

CLOCK IN / CLOCK OUT
  Workers clock in and out with a single tap. The app records the exact time,
  the project, and optionally the worker's GPS location. No paper timesheets,
  no calling in.

GPS GEOFENCING
  Admins can set a job site location and radius for any project. Workers will
  only be able to clock in if they are physically within that radius. Prevents
  buddy punching and off-site clock-ins.

REQUIRED SAFETY CHECKLIST ON CLOCK-IN
  Admins can require workers to complete a specific safety checklist before
  they are allowed to clock in — either globally (applies to all clock-ins)
  or on a per-project basis. If the worker hasn't submitted the checklist
  today, clock-in is blocked until they do.

BREAK AND MILEAGE TRACKING
  While clocked in, workers can log a break (in minutes) and job-related mileage
  before clocking out. Both are captured on the time entry.

CANCEL A WRONG CLOCK-IN
  If a worker accidentally clocks in to the wrong project, they can cancel the
  clock-in without creating any time entry, then clock in correctly.

MANUAL TIME ENTRY
  Workers can also log time manually — useful for entering hours after the fact.
  Fields: project, date, start time, end time, break, mileage, and notes.

EDIT AND DELETE ENTRIES
  Workers can edit or delete their own entries up to 7 days after the work date.
  Once approved by an admin, entries are locked and cannot be changed.

TIMESHEET VIEW
  A calendar-style view of a worker's own entries, grouped by week, so they can
  see their schedule at a glance.

COPY LAST WEEK
  Workers can copy all time entries from the previous week into the current week
  with a single tap — useful for workers who work the same projects and hours
  week over week.

TIMESHEET SIGN-OFF
  Workers can digitally sign off on their timesheet for a date range, indicating
  the hours are accurate. This timestamp is visible to admins in the approval
  queue.

PAY STUBS
  Workers can view their own pay stubs for each pay period — showing regular
  hours, overtime hours, prevailing wage hours, mileage, and estimated gross pay.

PDF TIMESHEET EXPORT
  Workers can export their own timesheet as a print-ready PDF from any date
  range, useful for personal records or disputes.

UPCOMING SHIFTS
  Workers see their scheduled shifts for the coming days. A single tap
  pre-fills the time entry form with the shift's project, date, and hours,
  so logging time is even faster.

TIME OFF REQUESTS
  Workers can submit time off requests directly from their dashboard, selecting
  a date range and adding a note. Admins are notified and can approve or deny
  the request from the admin panel.

EXPENSE / REIMBURSEMENT REQUESTS
  Workers can submit out-of-pocket expenses for reimbursement — amount, category,
  and a receipt photo. Admins review and approve, keeping job costs and employee
  reimbursements on the same platform.


================================================================================
ADMIN — APPROVAL AND OVERSIGHT
================================================================================

APPROVAL QUEUE
  All submitted time entries land in the admin's approval queue. Admins can
  approve or reject entries one at a time, or approve all entries at once.
  Rejections include an optional reason note that is visible to the worker.

FILTER BY WORKER
  When the queue has entries from multiple workers, admins can filter to a
  single worker and approve or reject just that person's entries.

APPROVE ALL (BULK)
  A single button approves every pending entry, or every pending entry for a
  specific worker, instantly.

TIME OFF MANAGEMENT
  Admins see all pending time off requests in one place and can approve or
  deny each request. Workers are notified of the decision automatically.

LIVE DASHBOARD (REFRESHES EVERY MINUTE)
  Admins see real-time KPI cards: how many workers are clocked in right now,
  total company hours this week, pending approvals, and workers approaching or
  in overtime. Updates automatically without refreshing the page.

LIVE WORKER STATUS
  A live list of which workers are currently clocked in, what project they're
  on, and how long they've been on the clock.

COMMENTS ON TIME ENTRIES
  Admins and workers can exchange threaded comments on any individual time
  entry — useful for clarifying questions, corrections, or notes before
  approval.

BROADCAST ANNOUNCEMENTS
  Admins can send a push notification to every active worker at once — for job
  site updates, schedule changes, urgent notices, or anything else that needs
  to reach the whole team immediately.

UNUSUAL CLOCK-IN ALERTS
  If a worker clocks in outside of configured working hours (e.g., 2 AM), the
  admin automatically receives an email alert.

INACTIVE WORKER ALERTS
  The system sends the admin a daily push notification and email if any worker
  has not submitted a time entry in a configurable number of days (default: 3).
  Helps catch workers who may have stopped logging time without notice.

OVERTIME ALERTS
  Admins receive an alert when a worker is approaching or has exceeded their
  overtime threshold, so they can act before labor costs spike.

AUDIT LOG
  Every significant admin action (approval, rejection, rate change, pay period
  lock/unlock, etc.) is recorded in an audit log with the actor's name and
  timestamp. Filterable by action group and date range.

SCHEDULING AND DISPATCH
================================================================================

SHIFT SCHEDULING
  Admins can create shifts and assign them to workers and projects, with a
  specific date and start/end time. Drag-and-drop calendar interface.

WORKER SHIFT VIEW
  Workers see their upcoming shifts on their dashboard and can tap to auto-fill
  a time entry from any shift.

ONLINE BOOKING
  Define appointment or shift types and which team members are bookable, then
  let customers book time online. Bookings flow into the schedule, and customers
  can view or manage their own appointment.

SERVICE REQUESTS
  Customers can submit service calls / job requests through a public intake form,
  so incoming work lands directly in the system instead of a voicemail or inbox.

PUBLIC COMPANY PROFILE
  Each company gets a public-facing profile page — a simple, shareable presence
  that ties together the booking and service-request intake.

PAYROLL AND COMPLIANCE
================================================================================

OVERTIME CALCULATION
  The system automatically calculates regular and overtime hours for each
  worker. Supports both daily overtime (e.g., over 8 hours in a day) and weekly
  overtime (e.g., over 40 hours in a week). Threshold and multiplier are
  configurable per company.

PREVAILING WAGE TRACKING
  Projects can be flagged as "prevailing wage." Hours on those projects are
  tracked separately from regular hours, with a configurable prevailing wage
  rate. Prevailing wage hours do not count toward overtime.

PAY PERIODS
  Admins can create pay periods (e.g., bi-weekly, semi-monthly). Once a pay
  period is locked, entries within it cannot be edited or deleted by workers.
  Pay periods appear in workers' pay stub views.

CERTIFIED PAYROLL REPORT
  Generates a weekly certified payroll report (WH-347 format) — required by law
  for contractors on Davis-Bacon / public projects paying prevailing wages.
  Shows hours worked each day of the week (Mon–Sun), total hours, work
  classification (regular vs. prevailing), pay rate, and gross wages per worker.
  Printable and saveable as a PDF.

OVERTIME REPORT
  A summary report showing regular hours, overtime hours, prevailing hours,
  total hours, and estimated labor cost per worker for any date range.

PAYROLL CSV EXPORT
  Export a full payroll summary as a CSV — regular pay, overtime pay, prevailing
  pay, total cost, and mileage per worker — for import into payroll software
  (ADP, Gusto, Paychex, etc.).

TIME ENTRY CSV EXPORT
  Export a detailed CSV of every individual time entry for any date range, with
  filters for worker, project, and approval status. Opens directly in Excel or
  Google Sheets.

QUICKBOOKS ONLINE INTEGRATION
  Connect to QuickBooks Online via OAuth. Push time and payroll data directly
  from OpsFloa into QuickBooks, eliminating double-entry. Supports employee
  mapping between the two systems. (QBO add-on.)

WORKER PAY STUBS
  Each worker can view a read-only pay stub for each pay period, showing their
  hour breakdown and estimated gross pay, calculated using their assigned hourly
  rate and the company's overtime settings.

ANALYTICS AND REPORTING
================================================================================

ANALYTICS DASHBOARD (ADMIN)
  Visual charts and summary stats:
    - Hours logged this week and this month
    - Number of active workers this week and this month
    - Pending approvals count
    - Daily bar chart — hours logged each day for the last 14 days
    - Weekly trend line — hours per week for the last 12 weeks
    - Top projects by hours logged (last 30 days)
    - Top workers by hours logged (last 30 days)

WORKER METRICS
  Per-worker breakdown showing their total hours, overtime, prevailing wage
  hours, and mileage over a date range — useful for performance review and
  labor cost analysis.

PROJECT REPORTS
  Per-project breakdown of hours logged, broken down by worker and date range.

LABOR COST ESTIMATES
  The overtime report and payroll export include estimated labor costs using
  each worker's assigned hourly rate and the company's overtime multiplier.

FINANCIAL REPORTS
  Company-level financials: a Profit & Loss view and a Work-in-Progress (WIP)
  report, so owners can see where the business — not just each job — stands.


================================================================================
PROJECTS
================================================================================

PROJECT MANAGEMENT
  Admins create and manage projects with a full set of fields: name, client,
  job number, address, start and end dates, status, wage type, description,
  progress percentage, and budget. Projects can be archived when complete.

BUDGET TRACKING
  Each project can have a budget set in dollars. The project detail panel
  shows a live budget bar — hours logged against budget — and a progress
  percentage. Admins can set a budget alert threshold to be notified when
  a project is approaching its limit.

PROJECT OVERVIEW
  Each project has a detail panel with:
    - Health status indicators (on track, at risk, over budget)
    - Punchlist items (view and create inline)
    - Document storage (upload, view, delete project documents)
    - Photo gallery for the project
    - RFIs associated with the project
    - Assigned workers roster
    - Budget and progress bars
    - Activity feed

BILLING TAB
  Per-project billing view showing hours and labor costs for a selected date
  range, broken down by worker and wage type. Export to CSV or generate a
  formatted PDF invoice with company letterhead, bill-to information, and a
  time entries table.

PROJECT TIME ENTRIES
  View all time entries logged against a project in one place, filterable by
  date.

DOCUMENT STORAGE
  Upload and manage project documents (plans, permits, contracts, etc.) stored
  securely in the cloud. Documents can be downloaded or deleted at any time.

PROJECT CLOSEOUT
  A closeout view per project for assembling and tracking the documentation a
  job needs to be formally closed out and handed over.


================================================================================
ESTIMATING, BIDDING AND CONTRACT DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

ESTIMATES AND BIDS
  Build estimates and bids from line items (description, quantity, unit, price),
  with company branding. Each estimate can carry a bid due date with reminders,
  so nothing is missed on a deadline.

PRICE CATALOG
  A reusable catalog of priced items and services, so estimates and bids are
  built from a consistent cost book instead of re-typing prices every time.

ATTACH THE PLAN SET
  Attach the plan PDF directly to an estimate, keeping the drawings and the
  pricing together.

TAKE OFF IN PLAN ROOM
  Launch an estimate's plan set straight into the Plan Room takeoff tools (see
  Tools, below). Measured quantities and priced takeoff lines flow back onto the
  estimate — measure the plans, and the numbers land on the bid.

SEND AND ACCEPT ONLINE
  Send an estimate to the client, who can review and accept it online through a
  public link — no account required.

CONVERT BID TO JOB
  Turn a winning bid into a live job/project in one step, carrying the details
  across so field work, time, and billing all connect back to the original bid.

CHANGE ORDERS
  Create change orders against a project with line items, and send them to the
  client for online review and signature via a public link. Keeps scope changes
  documented and approved.

SUBMITTALS
  Track submittals — product data, shop drawings, samples — through their review
  cycle, so approvals are logged and nothing stalls.

LIEN WAIVERS
  Generate lien waivers and send them out for online signature through a public
  link, keeping payment paperwork moving.

SUBCONTRACTORS
================================================================================

SUBCONTRACTOR DIRECTORY AND POs
  Maintain a subcontractor directory and issue numbered subcontractor purchase
  orders (POs) with line items — a clear paper trail for the work you hand off.

SUBCONTRACTOR REPORTS
  Document subcontractor work on site (in the Field app) — who was on site, what

================================================================================
CLIENTS
================================================================================

CLIENT MANAGEMENT
  Admins maintain a client directory with contact information: company name,
  contact person, email, and phone. Clients can be linked to projects so
  billing and reporting are always connected to the right company.

CLIENT DOCUMENT TRACKING
  For each client, track key compliance and legal documents: Certificate of
  Insurance (COI), W-9, W-2, contract, and business license. Each document
  has an optional expiry date. The system flags documents that are expired
  or expiring within 30 days, so nothing lapses without notice.

DOCUMENT UPLOAD
  Documents are uploaded directly to secure cloud storage and linked to the
  client record. All documents are available for download at any time.


================================================================================
FIELD APP
================================================================================

The Field App is a connected app (same login) for job site documentation.
Workers and admins switch between the Time Clock, Field, and other modules from
any screen.

FIELD REPORTS
  Workers submit daily field reports — notes, observations, issues — tied to a
  specific date and GPS location. Reports can include photos. Admins can mark
  reports as reviewed.

DAILY REPORTS
  Structured daily job site reports with a title, date, description, and photos.
  Admins can view, review, and download reports as PDFs.

PHOTO AND VIDEO CAPTURE
  Workers can take or attach photos and videos directly from their phone within
  the Field app and attach them to reports.

PUNCHLIST
  Create and track punch list items for any project — open issues, deficiencies,
  or tasks that need to be resolved. Items can be broken down into sub-tasks
  with a checklist, assigned to individuals, and marked complete.

TOOLBOX TALKS (SAFETY TALKS)
  Record toolbox talk / safety meeting details: title, content, who gave the
  talk, date, and project. Creates a digital record of safety training for
  compliance purposes.

SAFETY CHECKLISTS
  Create reusable safety checklist templates (daily pre-task, fall protection,
  hazard assessment, etc.). Workers complete and submit checklists from their
  device. Submissions are timestamped and stored for compliance records.
  Checklists can be required before a worker is allowed to clock in.

INSPECTION CHECKLISTS
  Structured site inspection forms that capture checklist responses by category.
  Useful for quality control walkthroughs, subcontractor inspections, and
  owner punch walks.

INCIDENT REPORTS
  Workers and admins can document job site incidents — injuries, near-misses,
  property damage — with a description, date, and supporting details. Records
  are retained for safety compliance and insurance purposes.

RFI TRACKING
  Submit, track, and manage Requests for Information (RFIs) from within the
  Field app or from a project's detail panel. Each RFI has a title, description,
  status, and response field.

HAUL / PRODUCTION LOG
  Log haul tickets on a job — loads, material, and quantities — with running
  totals and subtotals. On jobs that came from a bid, an estimate-vs-actual
  view reconciles what was bid against what was actually hauled.

TOOLS — PLAN ROOM, TAKEOFF, AND OFFICE
================================================================================

The Tools module is a set of specialized utilities that sit alongside the daily
workflow. The plan tools are paid add-ons; the office tools ride with the base.

PLAN ROOM (add-on)
  A browser-based plan viewer, markup, and measure tool.
    - Open a plan set — PDF or aerial image — with fast, multi-page navigation.
    - Mark it up: clouds, callouts, highlights, arrows, lines, and text notes.
    - Measure to scale: lengths, areas, and counts, after calibrating the scale
      from any known dimension (with an on-canvas, editable scale bar).
    - Company library: share a plan set to the whole company; copy it down on
      any device.
    - Live share sessions: co-edit a plan set with teammates in real time.
    - Export a flattened PDF of the marked-up set.
    - Work autosaves as you go.

TAKEOFF LAYER (add-on)
  Turns Plan Room measurements into priced, branded bids. Eleven trades, all on
  the same plan set, all feeding one bid — pick the trade and its tools appear.

  A general contractor takes off the whole building. A site contractor takes off
  the whole site. Nobody buys a second seat of anything.

  THE SITE
    - Earthwork / sitework: cut-and-fill from contours, spot elevations, and
      pads; two-sheet existing/proposed alignment with a ghost overlay; a cut/
      fill heat overlay; export cubic yards.
    - Erosion & sediment control: silt fence and perimeter controls by the foot,
      inlet protection and other BMPs by the each, and stabilized areas —
      construction entrance and riprap to stone tonnage, erosion blanket to
      square yards, hydroseed to seed and mulch. (Every grading permit needs an
      ESC sheet; it's the same plan set you already have open.)
    - Demolition: buildings and pavement to debris cubic yards, tons, and truck
      loads — buildings by construction type, pavement by thickness and swell.
      Plus curb, pipe, fence and guardrail removals, and trees, poles and
      structures by the each.
    - Parking-lot striping & signage: stalls by type with the ADA count called
      out separately, painted runs by width (4" line through 24" stop bar), and
      arrows, legends, signs, wheel stops and bollards. Paint and glass beads
      figured off the width-weighted footage.
    - Fencing & guardrail: runs by the foot with the post count worked out per
      run at each type's own spacing — chain link, wood, vinyl, ornamental,
      farm, W-beam guardrail, cable rail — plus gates and post concrete.
    - Landscape & irrigation: mulch by the yard, sod by the square, decorative
      rock by the ton, seed by the pound; trees, shrubs and perennials counted;
      irrigation mainline, laterals and drip by the foot, with heads, valves,
      controller and backflow by the each.

  THE BUILDING
    - Roofing: roof planes with pitch → squares; roof edges (eave, rake, ridge,
      valley, hip); roof items/accessories; aerial-image input for re-roofs.
    - Framing & lumber: wall runs to stud counts, plates and board-feet by stud
      size; door and window openings to headers and king/jack/cripple studs;
      sheathing to 4×8 sheets.
    - Siding, gutters & insulation: elevations to net square feet and squares by
      material (vinyl, fiber cement, wood, stucco, brick and stone veneer) with
      window and door openings deducted; gutters and downspouts by the foot;
      insulation by R-value, with batts to bags.
    - Drywall & paint: wall runs (length × height × sides), ceilings, openings
      (with SF deducts), and trim; board / mud / tape / paint quantities;
      texture and batt/sound insulation; ACT drop-ceiling grid takeoff (tiles,
      tees, wall angle, hangers); and wall heights measured directly off
      elevation sheets.
    - Flooring & tile: rooms to net floor SF by material with waste; thresholds,
      reducers and transitions by the foot; underlayment; and tile materials —
      thinset and grout figured from tile size and joint width.

  EVERY TRADE
    - Double-click anything to change its type; quantities re-figure instantly.
    - Same-type work rolls up into one bid line; different types stay separate.
    - Rates are seeded with sensible defaults and every one is editable.
    - Priced takeoff lines flow into a shared price library and a bid report,
      and can be sent straight back onto an estimate.
    - The tool refuses to measure an uncalibrated sheet rather than quietly
      handing you a zero.

STORM / UTILITY (add-on, on top of Takeoff)
  The deep version of the underground takeoff for utility contractors: a pipe
  schedule by diameter and material with automatic trench widths, invert-driven
  trench depth that varies along the run (rather than one flat depth per line),
  structure depth priced by vertical foot, and spoil-versus-import netting so
  the export number is the real one.

SITEWORK TAKEOFF ESTIMATOR
  A dedicated civil takeoff tool for taking off earthwork, paving, concrete,
  utilities, and site quantities from a plan set into a priced, branded bid.

OFFICE TOOLS (included)
    - Voice transcription — upload a meeting or call recording and get back a
      speaker-separated transcript.
    - Meeting & call summarizer — turn a transcript or notes into a clean
      summary with key points and action items.
    - Document Q&A — open a contract, spec, or insurance cert and ask questions
      answered only from the document.
    - Contract red-flag scanner — upload a subcontract and get back the terms
      that carry real money: pay-if-paid, retainage, the short notice windows
      that quietly waive your right to claim, liquidated damages, one-sided
      indemnity, open-ended scope. Ranked worst first, each with the actual
      clause quoted and the edit to ask for. A reading aid, not legal advice.
    - Email & message drafter — turn a few notes into a polished email or text
      in the tone you pick.
    - PDF Toolkit — merge, reorder, rotate, delete, and extract PDF pages,
      entirely on your device (nothing is uploaded).

COMPANY COMMUNICATION
================================================================================

COMPANY CHAT
  A company-wide group chat where workers and admins can communicate in real
  time. Messages are retained for a configurable number of days. Accessible
  from the worker dashboard.

PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
  Workers and admins can opt in to browser push notifications (works on iOS,
  Android, and desktop) to receive alerts for:
    - Approvals and rejections of their time entries
    - Comments on their entries from an admin
    - Broadcast announcements from admin
    - Time off request decisions (worker)
    - New time off requests (admin)
    - Budget alerts on projects (admin)
    - Inactive worker alerts (admin only)
    - Worker timesheet sign-offs (admin only)

ADMIN COMMENTS ON ENTRIES
  Admins can leave comments on individual time entries, visible to the worker.

PLATFORM AND TECHNICAL
================================================================================

WORKS ON ANY DEVICE
  Fully responsive web app — works on iPhone, Android, tablet, and desktop. No
  app store download required. Workers can add it to their home screen for a
  native-app feel.

INSTALLABLE (PWA)
  OpsFloa is a Progressive Web App. Workers can install it to their phone
  home screen and launch it like a native app. An in-app prompt guides them
  through installation on both iOS and Android.

OFFLINE MODE
  Clock-ins and clock-outs are queued locally if the worker loses internet
  connection. Punches sync automatically when connectivity is restored — no
  data is lost.

BILINGUAL: ENGLISH AND SPANISH
  The worker-facing Time Clock app is fully available in English and Spanish.
  Workers can switch languages from their dashboard. All UI labels, buttons,
  and status messages are translated.

SELF-SERVICE REGISTRATION
  New companies sign up online — choose a company name, create an admin
  account, and start adding workers immediately. No sales call or setup required.

FREE TRIAL
  New companies get a free trial period (configurable, default 14 days) before
  being prompted to subscribe. All features — including add-ons — are available
  during the trial.

WORKER INVITATIONS
  Admins invite workers by name and username. Workers receive an email with a
  link to set their own password. No admin handles worker passwords.

PASSWORD RESET AND EMAIL CONFIRMATION
  Standard forgot-password email flow. New admin accounts require email
  confirmation before logging in.

MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
  Workers and admins can enable time-based one-time password (TOTP) MFA on
  their account for an extra layer of login security, using any authenticator
  app (Google Authenticator, Authy, etc.).

ROLE-BASED ACCESS
  Three roles: Worker (can clock in/out, submit entries, view own data),
  Admin (full company management), Super Admin (platform-level access for the
  operator). Admin permissions are further configurable per worker.

MODULE TOGGLES
  Admins can enable or disable entire feature modules per company — Time Clock,
  Field, Projects, Analytics, Inventory, and Tools — so the app only shows what
  each company actually uses.

FEATURE FLAGS
  Within enabled modules, admins can individually toggle specific features:
  scheduling, analytics, company chat, prevailing wage tracking, project
  integration, overtime calculation, geolocation, media gallery, and more.

SUBSCRIPTION BILLING (STRIPE)
  Base plans (Free, Starter, Business) plus à-la-carte add-ons — QuickBooks
  Online, Plan Room, and the Takeoff layer — billed via Stripe. Admins manage
  their subscription and add-ons from within the app, including the Stripe
  customer portal to update payment methods, download invoices, or cancel.
  Stripe webhooks keep subscription status in sync automatically. Annual billing
  saves two months on the base plans.

COMPANY SETTINGS
  Admins configure:
    - Default hourly rate and prevailing wage rate
    - Overtime rule (daily or weekly), threshold, and multiplier
    - Currency (USD, CAD, EUR, GBP, MXN, and more)
    - Company timezone
    - Inactive worker alert threshold (days)
    - Unusual clock-in alert window (e.g., only alert outside 6 AM – 8 PM)
    - Chat message retention (days)
    - Media retention period
    - Invoice signature requirement (none, optional, required)
    - Default temporary password for new workers
    - Global required safety checklist for clock-in
    - Notification preferences (per event type)
    - Module and feature toggles

MEDIA STORAGE
  Photos and documents are stored securely in Cloudflare R2 cloud storage.
  Admins can configure a media retention period, and optionally auto-delete
  media when a project is archived.

SECURITY
  - All data scoped to the company — no company can see another company's data
  - Rate limiting on login and sensitive auth endpoints
  - Helmet security headers on all API responses
  - JWT-based authentication with token expiry
  - MFA support via TOTP authenticator apps
  - Approved time entries are locked and cannot be modified
  - Username availability is scoped per company
