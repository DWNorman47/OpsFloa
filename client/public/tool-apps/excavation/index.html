How to do a takeoff

  1. Open PDF… and load your civil set. Pick the page number for the Existing Conditions sheet and the Grading Plan sheet in the sidebar.
  2. 📏 Scale: click two points a known distance apart (a dimension, the scale bar, a property line with a labeled length) and type the distance in feet. Once is enough.
  3. ▱ Boundary: click around the limits of disturbance and press Enter to close. Only dirt inside this polygon is counted.
  4. 🪄 Auto Trace (fastest, vector PDFs): click once ON a contour and the whole line is lifted from the PDF's vector data — confirm the highlighted line looks right, then confirm its elevation (pre-filled from the nearest printed label when one is close by). One click per contour. With a boundary drawn, the picked line is trimmed to the boundary plus a small margin.
  5. 〰 Trace Contour (fallback, works on any PDF including scans) on the Existing tab: click, click, click along each contour — the wavy lines labeled with elevations (line style varies by engineer; ignore the straight dashed property/easement lines) — press Enter, type the elevation (308…), repeat. The elevation box pre-fills with the next value so you can fly through them.
  6. Switch to the Proposed tab and trace the design contours the same way (319, 318, 317…) — usually the dark solid lines; faint screened lines there are the existing ground for reference. Draw ▬ Pad polygons around building footprints (FF/FP pads) — everything inside is held flat at the elevation you enter. Use ◎ Spot Elev for individual spot grades.
  7. ∑ Calculate Cut/Fill. The app grids both surfaces inside the boundary, subtracts them, and gives you cut, fill, and net export/import in cubic yards, plus a heatmap of where the dirt moves.

Everything each tool can do

  • Moving around (any tool): wheel = zoom (adjust Zoom speed % in Settings for finer steps) · right-drag, middle-drag, or Space+drag = pan.
  • Drawing lines/polygons (Trace, Boundary, Pad): click points · Enter or double-click finishes · Backspace removes the last click · Esc cancels.
  • 📏 Scale: two clicks + distance in feet. With the tool active afterwards: drag an endpoint (○) to fine-tune it, click two new points to re-measure, or use the sidebar's Edit distance… to change the feet without moving the points.
  • ▱ Boundary editing (Boundary tool active, polygon closed): drag a point (○) to move it · double-click an edge to add a point · Alt+click a point to remove it · click away from the polygon to draw a replacement.
  • 🪄 Auto Trace: one click ON a line lifts the whole vector path (vector PDFs only — it will tell you if the page is a scan). Cancel the elevation prompt to discard a bad pick. With a boundary drawn, picks are trimmed to the boundary plus ~20 ft.
  • ▬ Pad editing (Pad tool active): same gestures as the boundary — drag a point (○), double-click an edge to add, Alt+click to remove. Enter the pad/subgrade elevation (often FF minus slab & base), not the finished floor. Select a pad by clicking anywhere inside it.
  • ➤ Select: click a line (or its row in the sidebar list) · Delete removes it · E edits its elevation · Esc deselects. The sidebar list's ✕ also deletes.
  • ⌫ Erase Box: drag a box — only the line portions inside are erased; lines crossing the box are split and the outside parts survive. With a line selected, only that line is trimmed. Spots inside are removed; pads are only removed when fully boxed.
  • ⌖ Align sheets (sidebar): turn on Ghost other sheet to check the two pages overlay. If not: click a landmark on Existing, the same one on Proposed (fixes the shift), then press Enter to stop — or click a second, far-away landmark pair to also fix rotation. Reset removes the adjustment.
  • ↶ Undo (Ctrl+Z): steps back through the last 50 changes — traces, erases, boundary/pad/scale/alignment edits, Clear, Load, even New.
  • 📁 Projects: each project is a separate takeoff. Duplicate makes a second version on the same plans (shared PDF, independent traces) — ideal for comparing grading options. Open, rename, or delete from the list; the current one shows in the toolbar. Switching flushes and saves automatically; Undo history doesn't cross projects.
  • Saving: everything auto-saves in the browser, including the PDF — a refresh reopens the current project. New wipes the current project's takeoff (keeps the PDF open). Save/Load exports/imports a .takeoff.json (the PDF itself is not embedded — keep it with the file).
  • Sidebar: click any section header to collapse/expand it, and ☰ in the toolbar hides the whole sidebar (the app remembers both). Shrink/swell % in Settings re-figure the results live after a calculation.
  • Small screens: the toolbar stacks into rows — the tool buttons become a side-scrolling strip, with Calculate and the file actions on their own rows. ☰ frees up the whole screen for the drawing.
