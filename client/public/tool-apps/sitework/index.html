How to do a takeoff

  1. Open PDF… and load your civil set. Pick the page number for the Existing Conditions sheet and the Grading Plan sheet in the sidebar.
  2. 📏 Scale: click two points a known distance apart (a dimension, the scale bar, a property line with a labeled length) and type the distance in feet. Once is enough.
  3. ▱ Boundary: click around the limits of disturbance and press Enter to close. Only dirt inside this polygon is counted.
  4. 🪄 Auto Trace (fastest, vector PDFs): click once ON a contour and the whole line is lifted from the PDF's vector data — confirm the highlighted line looks right, then confirm its elevation (pre-filled from the nearest printed label when one is close by). One click per contour. With a boundary drawn, the picked line is trimmed to the boundary plus a small margin.
  5. 〰 Trace Contour (fallback, works on any PDF including scans) on the Existing tab: click, click, click along each contour — the wavy lines labeled with elevations (line style varies by engineer; ignore the straight dashed property/easement lines) — press Enter, type the elevation (308…), repeat. The elevation box pre-fills with the next value so you can fly through them.
  6. Switch to the Proposed tab and trace the design contours the same way (319, 318, 317…) — usually the dark solid lines; faint screened lines there are the existing ground for reference. Draw ▬ Pad polygons around building footprints (FF/FP pads) — everything inside is held flat at the elevation you enter. Use ◎ Spot Elev for individual spot grades.
  7. ∑ Calculate Cut/Fill. The app grids both surfaces inside the boundary, subtracts them, and gives you cut, fill, and net export/import in cubic yards, plus a heatmap of where the dirt moves.

Everything each tool can do

  • Moving around (any tool): wheel = zoom (adjust Zoom speed % in Settings for finer steps) · right-drag, middle-drag, or Space+drag = pan.
  • Drawing lines/polygons (Trace, Boundary, Pad): click points · Enter or double-click finishes · Backspace removes the last click · Esc cancels.
  • 📏 Scale: two clicks + distance in feet. With the tool active afterwards: drag an endpoint (○) to fine-tune it, click two new points to re-measure, or use the sidebar's Edit distance… to change the feet without moving the points.
  • ▱ Boundary editing (Boundary tool active, polygon closed): drag a point (○) to move it · double-click an edge to add a point · Alt+click a point to remove it · click away from the polygon to draw a replacement.
  • 🪄 Auto Trace: one click ON a line lifts the whole vector path (vector PDFs only — it will tell you if the page is a scan). Cancel the elevation prompt to discard a bad pick. With a boundary drawn, picks are trimmed to the boundary plus ~20 ft.
  • ▬ Pad editing (Pad tool active): same gestures as the boundary — drag a point (○), double-click an edge to add, Alt+click to remove. Enter the pad/subgrade elevation (often FF minus slab & base), not the finished floor. Select a pad by clicking anywhere inside it.
  • ▚ Wall (retaining-wall dig): click along the wall line · Enter/double-click finishes · then enter the trench cross-section (bottom width, side slope, and either a constant depth or a footing subgrade elevation read off your Existing contours). You get excavation and export CY per wall in the sidebar. Or use 📐 Wall Dig in the toolbar for a quick estimate with no plans.
  • ▦ Area (quantity takeoff): click around an area — paving, aggregate base, concrete, gravel, seeding · Enter/double-click closes it · then pick a material preset or set the output (area SF/SY, volume CY, or weight tons) and a thickness. Choose Concrete for volume plus edge-form LF and rebar/mesh, Asphalt paving for tonnage plus tack coat, or Topsoil strip for strip volume with swell and optional respread → net export CY. Quantities collect in the Quantities sidebar panel.
  • ╱ Line (linear takeoff): click along a run — curb, pipe/utility, silt fence, sawcut, fence · Enter/double-click finishes · gives length in feet. Tick Trench cross-section (pipe/utility) for excavation-spoil CY plus aggregate-bedding CY. Collects in the Quantities panel.
  • ⊕ Count (count takeoff): click each item — catch basins, manholes, trees, signs, light poles · Enter/double-click finishes · then name it. Gives an each (EA) tally in the Quantities panel.
  • $ Bid (report): rolls every measured quantity — cut/fill, wall digs, and area/line/count takeoffs — into one priced table. Type a unit price per line; add overhead & profit for the total. Prices save with the project and are remembered across projects, pre-filling matching line items next time. Add your logo + company block for a branded letterhead, and the haul line turns export CY into truck loads at your truck capacity. Export CSV for a spreadsheet, or Print / Save PDF for a clean one-page bid.
  • 🗒 Log (production log): once the job is running, record haul tickets (date, ticket #, hauler, material, qty) and daily reports (weather, crew, hours, work performed) against this project. It tallies hauled CY against your estimated export and totals man-hours, with CSV and Print / Save PDF export. Saved with the project.
  • 📐 Measure (Scale section, sidebar): click two points to read the straight-line distance in feet — it doesn't change the scale, just a quick readout. A closed ▱ Boundary also shows its perimeter and acreage in the Boundary section.
  • ➤ Select / reshape: click a line (or its row in the sidebar list) to select it — its points show as ○ handles. Drag a point to move it · double-click an edge to add a point · Alt+click a point to remove it. Delete removes the whole line · E edits its elevation · Esc deselects. Works on traced contours, spot grades, and pads.
  • ⌫ Erase Box: drag a box — only the line portions inside are erased; lines crossing the box are split and the outside parts survive. With a line selected, only that line is trimmed. Spots inside are removed; pads are only removed when fully boxed.
  • ⌖ Align sheets (sidebar): turn on Ghost other sheet to check the two pages overlay. If not: click a landmark on Existing, the same one on Proposed (fixes the shift), then press Enter to stop — or click a second, far-away landmark pair to also fix rotation. Reset removes the adjustment.
  • ⌖ Re-align traces to this PDF (Revision align, sidebar): after opening a revised PDF where the drawing shifted, this moves your carried scale, boundary, and traces onto it. Click a landmark on a carried trace, then the same spot on the new drawing; Enter after one pair for a shift, or add a second far-away pair for rotation/scale. It re-derives ft/px automatically; Ctrl+Z undoes it. (This moves your work; Align sheets above only nudges the Proposed image.)
  • ↶ Undo (Ctrl+Z): steps back through the last 50 changes — traces, erases, boundary/pad/scale/alignment edits, Clear, Load, even New.
  • 📁 Projects: each project is a separate takeoff. Duplicate makes a second version on the same plans (shared PDF, independent traces) — ideal for comparing grading options. Revise… forks a takeoff into a new revision: it carries the scale, boundary, and traces forward, then you Open PDF… the updated plan set — the drawing swaps but your work stays, so you only edit what changed (re-check the scale if the new sheet prints at a different size). The original revision is left untouched. Open, rename, or delete from the list; the current one shows in the toolbar. Switching flushes and saves automatically; Undo history doesn't cross projects.
  • Saving: everything auto-saves in the browser, including the PDF — a refresh reopens the current project. New wipes the current project's takeoff (keeps the PDF open). Save/Load exports/imports a .takeoff.json (the PDF itself is not embedded — keep it with the file).
  • Sidebar: click any section header to collapse/expand it, and ☰ in the toolbar hides the whole sidebar (the app remembers both). Shrink/swell % in Settings re-figure the results live after a calculation.
  • Small screens: the toolbar stacks into rows — the tool buttons become a side-scrolling strip, with Calculate and the file actions on their own rows. ☰ frees up the whole screen for the drawing.

Projects

Each project is its own takeoff. Duplicate one to price a second version of the same plans — the PDF is shared, the traces are independent. Revise… starts a new revision that carries the scale, boundary, and traces forward so you can open the updated plans and edit only what changed.

Company takeoffs

Shared with everyone in your company. Open loads a teammate's takeoff — plan and all. Share current takeoff saves the one you're working on; if a teammate changed the shared copy since you opened it, you'll be warned before overwriting. Needs you to be signed in to OpsFloa.
